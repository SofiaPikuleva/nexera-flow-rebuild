What this is

We opened Nexera the way a new learner does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Nexera: installed it, went through the end of onboarding — the loader, your learning plan and the paywall — read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — the olive ring and your mark, the goal card and the plan curve, the Before / After illustration, your two plan cards with BEST OFFER and the black Continue. Only the order and a few lines changed, using things you already have but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this
