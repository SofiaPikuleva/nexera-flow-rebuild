# Growth Audit — Nexera

**Live:** https://sofiapikuleva.github.io/nexera-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for Nexera: Learn AI Tools, plus a clickable prototype of the end of onboarding and the paywall, rebuilt in Nexera's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026, from the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Put their goal on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | End the plan curve on Day 28 | Your plan | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | On close, offer your Annual Promo price | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Show what the year saves | Paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 05 | Name the goal while the path builds | Loader | CR +10–15% |

Every screen in the prototype is Nexera's own design. Only the sequence and a few lines changed, using things Nexera already has but does not show at the right moment.

## Grounding

Every price, programme length and claim comes from Nexera's own published material: the US App Store listing (Monthly Subscription USD 9.99, Yearly Subscription USD 69.99, Annual Promo Subscription USD 39.99, the 28-Day Structured AI Challenge and the AI certification) and the in-app screens (the selected-goal card, the plan curve, Before / After, BEST OFFER).

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Nexera.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000. Add `?open=proto` to open straight on the prototype.
